Baking Recipe Keeper

Product Roadmap & Monetization Plan

Sweet plans ahead ✨
Where we are 🍞 — A solid core: recipes with ingredients & baking procedure, metadata (servings / prep / bake / difficulty), live scaling, share, search & filters, a dashboard with drill-down, reorderable steps, light/dark theming with a caramel brand palette, snackbar feedback, and reliable Room storage with migrations.

🗺️Feature Roadmap (Phased)

Phase 1 — Complete the core app Free · Retention

  • Favorites ⭐ + Favorites tab/filter
  • Duplicate recipe (great base for variations)
  • Bottom navigation (Home · Favorites · Categories · More)
  • Shopping list — aggregate & de-duplicate ingredients into a checklist
  • Export & print — text, CSV, PDF
  • Media — multiple photos + a short procedure video, full-screen viewer
  • “Start Baking” mode — full-screen, screen-on, per-step timers
  • Backup / restore (JSON), empty-state art, onboarding, tags

Phase 2 — Accounts + Cloud Unlocks subscriptions

  • User accounts (Google / email)
  • Cloud sync & backup across devices
  • Unit conversion (g↔oz, ml↔cups) & smart scaling
  • Meal / bake planner + reminders
  • Import recipe from a URL, and photo/OCR → recipe

Phase 3 — Learning + Community Expands audience

  • “Learn to Bake” hub — techniques, glossary, tips, fixes
  • AI assistant — substitutions, troubleshooting, auto-scaling
  • Community — publish, rate, comment, follow, collections
  • Shareable web links + landing page (open in app / “Get the app”)

Phase 4 — Scale + Platforms Growth

  • Creator marketplace — sell/curate recipe packs (revenue share)
  • iOS + Web (Compose Multiplatform)
  • Localization (languages, metric/imperial)
  • Home-screen widgets, WearOS timers, voice hands-free

💰Monetization — Freemium + Subscription

Free 🆓

$0
  • ~15–25 recipes
  • Core editing & scaling
  • Search & share
  • Light ads (optional)

Premium 👑

~$3–5/mo · ~$25–30/yr
  • Unlimited recipes
  • Cloud sync & backup
  • Media/video, shopping list
  • PDF export, planner, AI
  • Premium themes · no ads

Lifetime / Family 🏡

~$40–60 once
  • One-time unlock option
  • Shared library (household)
  • Great for classes / cafés

Other revenue streams 🫙

  • Affiliate links on ingredients & tools (Amazon, baking supplies)
  • In-app purchases — recipe packs, seasonal collections, theme packs
  • Creator marketplace revenue share (Phase 4)
  • B2B — license to baking schools & classes
How: Google Play Billing for subscriptions, a paywall gating premium features, an isPremium check, and analytics to track conversion.

👩‍🍳Useful to Every Baker (& Cook)

  • Beginners: guided Start-Baking mode, glossary, technique videos, timers, AI “why did this fail?”
  • Experienced: scaling, unit conversion, yield math, notes & versioning, export/print
  • Cooks: broaden to savory recipes — a general recipe keeper with a baking heart
  • Everyone: cloud sync, shopping list, social sharing
